# AI interaction as observable work

[Back to README](../README.md)

## The prompt expresses a working formulation

The candidate chooses what context to supply, which uncertainty to investigate, what result to demand, and what authority to delegate. Those choices can be visible in prompts, but their diagnostic value depends on what follows.

A useful evidence unit contains:

1. Observation or unresolved requirement.
2. Claim and prediction.
3. Selected action or prompt.
4. Evidence actually obtained.
5. Revision or justified next step.

This captures an observable process without asserting access to private reasoning.

## Three legitimate modes

| Mode | Example | Evidence it can provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Broad delegation | Inspect the service, reproduce a defect, and repair it. | Effective use of an autonomous tool and the resulting artifact |
| Directed investigation | Check identity, expiry, and replay behavior before changing storage. | Prioritization and model construction when connected to the situation |
| Specific hypothesis | Construct the smallest trace in which an old claim affects a newer owner, then test the proposed atomic guard. | A causal model, prediction, discriminating experiment, and adjacent regression |

The third prompt may contain more visible reasoning. It is not automatically a higher-scoring strategy. AI can generate it, and broad delegation may produce an excellent verified outcome.

## What an interviewer can observe

- The candidate includes a material contract fact rather than an unsupported assumption.
- A response exposes uncertainty and the next action addresses it.
- A prediction is recorded before a result is revealed.
- A tool is used to execute a proposed check rather than merely describe one.
- Contradictory evidence changes the model or claim.
- The candidate recognizes the difference between a passing bounded check and a wider proof.
- The final handoff matches the artifact and observed evidence.

AI is allowed at each step, including while forming predictions. A single polished explanation is weak attribution evidence. The complete interaction remains evidence of assisted performance.

## Do not demand unnecessary performance

Use the common milestone probes instead of requiring narration during every tool call. Do not require an AI model's hidden reasoning or a complete private thought transcript.

Ask for concise claims, assumptions, predictions, results, and decisions. These are engineering artifacts and can be evaluated directly.

## Keep verification connected to the contract

Checks generated by the same agent can reproduce its mistaken assumption. Compare them to the published behavior, inspect the relevant observation, and seek a plausible counterexample. An additional agent's agreement alone is not an independent correctness argument.

The scenario guides identify checks with externally meaningful outcomes. Equivalent schemas and legal allocation choices must be accepted.

## Attribution limits

The human may supply a key context fact, choose a more useful experiment, correct an objective, or simply delegate efficiently. Record observable interventions and their effects.

When the human and an autonomous agent produce equivalent verified results, do not invent a difference based on style. Use [AI-only baselines](calibration.md) to assess whether the exercise still has enough resolution to distinguish performance.
