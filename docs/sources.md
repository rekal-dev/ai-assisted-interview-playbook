# Sources and scope

[Back to README](../README.md)

The pattern, 60-minute schedule, rubric anchors, and scenarios are proposals for piloting. These primary sources support background principles. They do not validate this particular assessment under unrestricted AI.

| Source | Relevance | Limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Leslie Lamport — A High-Level View of TLA+](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/tla/high-level-view.html) | State-based models, initial conditions, transitions, behaviors, and fairness | A modeling foundation, not evidence of interview validity |
| [Lamport — PlusCal tutorial Session 6](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/tla/tutorial/session6.html) | Invariants and reachable states | Model properties still depend on the chosen representation |
| [Lamport — PlusCal tutorial Session 9](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/tla/tutorial/session9.html) | Safety, liveness, and progress assumptions | Does not supply a performance or workload model |
| [OPM — Structured Interviews](https://www.opm.gov/policy-data-oversight/assessment-and-selection/structured-interviews/) | Common questions and rating standards | General interview guidance, not validation of this format |
| [OPM — Developing a Customized Rating Scale](https://www.opm.gov/frequently-asked-questions/assessment-policy-faq/structured-interviews/how-do-i-develop-a-customized-rating-scale-for-structured-interviews/) | Behavioral examples for rating levels and subject-matter involvement | The anchors here need role-specific piloting |
| [OPM — Scoring Structured Interviews](https://www.opm.gov/frequently-asked-questions/assessment-policy-faq/structured-interviews/how-do-i-score-a-structured-interview-how-do-i-assign-points-to-the-content-areas-and-rating-scale/) | Equal weighting absent a documented reason for alternatives | No universal hiring cutoff follows |
| [Tristan Hume — Designing AI-resistant technical evaluations](https://www.anthropic.com/research/AI-resistant-technical-evaluations) | January 2026 take-home redesigns; multiple evidence opportunities and the tension between novelty and realism | A dated team account; not a benchmark for these scenarios |
| [Felix Rieseberg — Hunger beats tenure](https://felixrieseberg.com/hunger-beats-tenure-advice-for-junior-engineers/) | October 2026 move toward demonstrated work and product judgment | One manager's account; not a company-wide interview policy |
| [Anthropic — How employment is changing at Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/employment-at-anthropic) | October 2026 internal study of attention bottlenecks and increased emphasis on autonomy and judgment | Observations within one company; does not validate this interview format |
| [Anthropic — Candidate AI guidance](https://www.anthropic.com/candidate-ai-guidance) | Published distinction between permitted preparation and assessment-specific permission for AI | This playbook explicitly permits AI throughout; employer rules differ |

Prefer links to the original material over summaries. Record the actual model and harness versions for any new empirical claim about this repository.
